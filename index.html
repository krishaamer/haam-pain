SCORING SYSTEM

100 points, then reality.

Scores are hypotheses. Paid behavior beats desk research every time.

01

Pain anatomy

Intensity, urgency, frequency, consequences, emotional load and confusion. High pain plus high confusion is especially interesting.

02

Economics

Existing spend, willingness to pay, value created or loss avoided, gross margin, repeatability and sales efficiency.

03

Founder fit

Capability match is scored separately from domain credibility. The framework does not assume expertise just because a topic appeared in recent work.

04

Evidence ladder

Complaint < search < spending < paying us < measurable result. Confidence rises only when behavior gets more expensive.

priority = (weighted score - risk penalty) × evidence adjustment

Risk is kept outside the 100-point upside score so regulation, liability, trust and operational complexity stay visible.
