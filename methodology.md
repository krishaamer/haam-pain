# HAAM Pain methodology

## Objective

Find opportunities at the intersection of severe human or organizational pain, real ability to pay, a non-obvious insight gap and a credible path for HAAM to create measurable value.

The score is for prioritizing investigation. It is not a market-size forecast or investment recommendation.

## Weighted score

| Dimension | Weight | Core question |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Pain | 24 | How badly does the buyer want this gone? |
| Economics | 20 | Can solving it create meaningful financial return? |
| Founder fit | 15 | Can HAAM become unusually good and credible here? |
| Distribution | 11 | Can buyers be reached at the right trigger moment? |
| Insight gap | 10 | Is the current explanation or solution meaningfully incomplete? |
| Solution leverage | 8 | Can a small intervention create a large result? |
| Market quality | 6 | Is the buyer pool durable enough? |
| Validation speed | 6 | How quickly can real behavior prove or kill the hypothesis? |

All dimensions use a 0 to 5 scale.

## Risk

Risk is subtracted after the upside score. This prevents a regulated or liability-heavy opportunity from looking deceptively safe just because it has strong demand.

Evaluate:

- regulatory, legal and clinical exposure
- trust and professional-credibility barriers
- capital intensity
- marketplace chicken-and-egg risk
- single-platform dependence
- incumbent retaliation
- ethical and reputational risk
- long time to measurable outcome

## Evidence confidence

Confidence uses a 0 to 5 scale and should be based on evidence quality, recency and triangulation.

Suggested anchors:

- 0: intuition only
- 1: anecdotes
- 2: several independent complaints or weak market data
- 3: official data plus current complaints
- 4: official data plus current complaints plus observed spending or competitors
- 5: direct paid experiments and measured outcomes

## Founder fit

Founder fit should not become a disguised preference score.

Score capability fit and domain credibility separately before collapsing them into the founder-fit dimension. A strong digital-product skill set can make an opportunity buildable while weak domain trust can still make the initial go-to-market difficult.

Current HAAM capability assumptions used for the Toronto baseline:

- end-to-end digital product design
- interaction and service design
- software prototyping
- AI integration and automation
- research and synthesis
- information architecture
- product strategy

Not assumed:

- clinical credentials
- legal representation credentials
- mortgage or insurance licensing
- caregiving credentials
- deep pre-existing reputation in Toronto trades or regulated professions

## Experiment rule

Do not build the full product first.

For each highly ranked pain, define the narrowest paid diagnostic or concierge intervention that can test:

1. whether the buyer feels the pain now
2. whether the buyer trusts the proposed diagnosis
3. whether they will pay before the solution is automated
4. whether the intervention produces an observable improvement

Only then increase software investment.
